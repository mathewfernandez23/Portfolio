StanHaus Logo

The StanHaus logo is a wordmark designed for a K-pop merchandise brand, built around a retro varsity/collegiate script style. This lettering approach — bold, looping cursive reminiscent of vintage letterman jackets and classic soda branding — was chosen because that same retro-sport aesthetic is already popular within K-pop fan culture (fan-club "varsity" jackets, lightstick branding), making it instantly recognizable to the target audience while still feeling fun and nostalgic rather than corporate.

Typographically, the two words "Stan" and "Haus" are stacked and rendered in custom hand-lettered script with exaggerated loops on the capital letters, giving the mark energy and flow while staying legible at small sizes for use on card packs, stickers, and tags. The color palette sticks to a single teal/jade green with a darker outline and offset shadow, creating a dimensional, sticker-like quality that echoes trading cards and enamel pins, while also keeping production simple across different print vendors.

The overall silhouette is an irregular, rounded badge shape rather than a hard geometric outline, reinforcing the collectible, sticker-adjacent feel and making the logo easy to adapt into square formats like social avatars or packaging stamps. Conceptually, the name "StanHaus" merges fandom slang ("stan") with a more structured, crafted connotation ("haus"), and the design brief aimed to capture that same balance — playful and fan-native, but still polished and intentional.
