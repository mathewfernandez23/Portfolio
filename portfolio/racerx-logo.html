RacerX Logo

Primary brand mark for RacerX, a gaming company; audience is millennial-leaning gamers (casual and serious) across mixed cultural backgrounds. Needed to read instantly as "gaming" while staying clean enough to scale down for app icons, merch, and packaging.

Concept builds the mark from a stylized game controller silhouette — plus-shaped D-pad and four round face buttons — so the icon communicates "gaming brand" without needing the wordmark to explain it. Deep blue paired with a soft mint conveys the brand's stated personality (playful, tech-savvy, minimalistic) while still holding its own against competitors' bolder blues, greens, and purples. The rounded, bold "RacerX" wordmark in mint echoes the controller's soft-edged shapes, tying icon and type into one cohesive system, with a black-and-white variant built in for low-contrast use.

Strongest logo-design exercise in the set — reducing a product category (gaming) to a single recognizable silhouette, then building a full usable color/mono system around it, is a more advanced identity-design skill than illustration or layout work alone.
