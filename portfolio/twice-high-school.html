TWICE High School Poster

A nostalgic yearbook-style fan poster for TWICE stans; needed to feel personal and playful, like a real superlatives page, while clearly reading each member's identity.

Concept is "K-pop idols as high schoolers" — varsity jacket photos, hand-drawn doodles, and collegiate block lettering sell the schoolyard nostalgia. Doodles are color-coded per member to hint at personality/fandom nicknames, reinforcing a warm, community-built feel.

Unique for the hand-drawn illustration layer over photography — a harder technique than flat text posters — and shows range beyond StanHaus's product-focused work into pure fan-culture storytelling.
