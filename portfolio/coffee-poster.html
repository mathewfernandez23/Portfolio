Community Cup - Grand Opening Poster

This poster was designed to promote the grand opening of a fictional neighborhood coffee shop, targeting local community members in their 20s-50s who value connection and small business culture. The intent was to communicate the opening date, location, introductory discount, and social media handle in a single glance while establishing a warm and welcoming brand personality.

Design best practices are evident through the place. The warm cream and gold analogous color palette immediately signals artisanal quality and warmth without relying on corporate aesthetics. The grid-based layout separates the composition into clear visual zones, illustrated icons on the left, information hierarchy on the right, creating organized readability.

The consistent circular motif used across both the illustrated icons and the lifestyle photography at the bottom applies the principle of repetition to unify the composition, while the combination of hand-drawn style typography and real photography adds a sophisticated, layered visual texture that most student work avoids.
