KATSEYE Card Pack Teaser (StanHaus)

This promotional teaser Instagram post was designed to market the KATSEYE card pack for StanHaus, showcasing six custom Pokemon trainer-style cards featuring the members of the group, KATSEYE. The target audience are K-pop fans who’re also familiar with the Pokemon trading card format, making the crossover concept immediately legible and desirable to the niche.

The dark purple gradient background is a direct visual reference to KATSEYE’s signature color palette, showing an understanding of group-specific brand identity that makes the product feel tailored rather than templated, It is also creating depth and drama that makes the fanned card arrangement pop visually, while the subtle KATSEYE logo and “STANHAUS” watermark text in the background layers establish brand ownership without competing with the card artwork.

The group’s circular logo and “KATSEYE” wordmark at the bottom create a clear focal anchor that grounds the composition, while the StanHaus logo badge in the lower corner mirrors real Pokemon set branding conventions, positioning the product as a legitimate collectible rather than informal fan art.
