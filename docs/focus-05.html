Focus 05 Downtown - Restaurant Advertisement

This wide-format banner ad was created to attract urban professionals and food-forward New Yorkers to a fictional upscale downtown restaurant. The goal was to communicate four key service features: mobile ordering, an alcohol menu, live entertainment, and healthy and organic options, while establishing a refined, modern brand presence that stands apart from typical fast food advertisement.

The muted sage green background is a deliberate choice that signals sophistication and health-consciousness without the aggressive energy of saturated colors. The symmetrical arrangement of circular lifestyle photos on both sides of the central hero food image applies the principles of balance and visual rhythm, creating a stable, editorial composition that feels intentional rather than crowded.

The restrained copy load respects the viewer’s time, while the distinctive macron character in “Focus” demonstrates typographic attention to detail that elevates the brand identity above generic restaurant marketing.
