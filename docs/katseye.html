LE SSERAFIM Card Pack Teaser (StanHaus)

This promotional post was designed to market the K-pop group LE SSERAFIM, featuring five custom trainer cards for the five-member K-Pop group. Like the KATSEYE post, it targets K-pop fans with Pokemon card familiarity, but the visual treatment is distinctly different to reflect LE SSERAFIM’s cooler and more refined brand aesthetic.

The light powder blue gradient background is, again like KATSEYE, a direct visual reference to LE SSERAFIM’s signature color palette. The arc arrangement of the five cards, fanned outward in a semicircle, mirrors the official Pokemon set promotional photography style, lending the product instant credibility and collectible appeal.

The jagged lightning bold design element used for the group name treatment draws from LE SSERAFIM’s own visual visual identity language, showing research into the group’s aesthetic rather than applying a generic title treatment.
