College Pizza - Mock Advertisement

This mock print advertisement was designed to promote a fictional fast food pizza brand to college students ages 18-24, emphasizing affordability, convenience, and lifestyle alignment through the tagline “The Ultimate Study Buddy.” The ad needed to communicate brand name, appetite appeal, and a clear call to action within a single glance.

The saturated red background employs color psychology consistent with major fast food brands. Red is proven to stimulate appetite and urgency, making it an intentional rather than decorative choice. The most sophisticated technique is the layered typographic background texture: oversized semi-transparent “PIZZA” letterforms behind the hero content add depth and reinforce the brand without cluttering the hierarchy, demonstrating an understanding of visual layering beyond basic layout.

The off-center pizza photo creates asymmetric balance and visual tension that keeps the composition dynamic, while the circular badge logo in the lower left follows standard advertising conventions for brand mark placement.
