ONCEUMerch Website

A live WooCommerce storefront for fanmade TWICE merchandise; audience is ONCEs (TWICE's fandom) shopping for stickers, posters, apparel, and keychains. Needed clear product categorization, pricing, and easy add-to-cart flow to actually convert browsers into buyers.

Concept leans into a soft, girly pastel palette and heart/wordmark branding that matches the fandom's aesthetic, with a nav structure (Badges, Clothing, Keychains, Mystery Box, Posters, Stickers) built around how fans actually shop by product type. "New Arrivals" and "Best Selling" sections up top build trust and social proof before asking for a purchase decision.

Shows full e-commerce build skills, not just static design — theming, product categorization, and cart/wishlist functionality on a live WooCommerce site is a different (and more technical) skill set than a poster or mockup.
