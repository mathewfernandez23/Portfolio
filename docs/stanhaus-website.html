StanHaus Website

A live e-commerce storefront (STILL IN PROGRESS) for StanHaus's premium K-pop x Pokémon collectible cards; audience is collectors looking for a more premium, brand-forward buying experience than a typical Etsy listing. Needed to immediately establish trust (real artists, no AI) and guide visitors to shop or learn the brand story.

Concept positions StanHaus as a step up from ONCEUMerch — cleaner typography, more whitespace, and a tagline-driven hero ("Fandom. Individuality. Collecting.") over a dense product grid, signaling a curated collectible brand rather than a high-volume merch shop. Clear "Shop Now" and "Our Story" CTAs split new visitors into buy-now vs. learn-more paths right away.

Demonstrates brand differentiation skill — building two separate storefronts under one creative umbrella that each feel distinct to their audience (collectible-premium vs. fandom-playful) is a stronger strategic exercise than a single site.
