Piddle Paddle Tours - Tri-Fold Brochure

This two-sided tri-fold brochure was designed to market a fictional coastal tour company to vacationers and families visiting Gulf Shores, Alabama. The piece needed to function as both an eye-catching standalone display item and a practical information guide covering five distinct tour experiences across six panels.

The coastal blue-gray palette and script typography immediately establish the brand’s aspirational, premium feel. The most sophisticated design technique employed is the diagonal photo-cropping across panel corners creates visual dynamism and prevents the multi-panel layout from feeling static or grid-locked.

The consistent use of script headings across both sides of the brochure ties the front and back into a unified brand experience, while the photo-attribution credits demonstrate professional awareness of licensing standards rarely seen in student portfolio work.
