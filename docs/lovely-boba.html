Lovely Boba Stickers V2 (ONCEUMerch)

An Instagram product-preview post for ONCEUMerch; audience is TWICE fans browsing merch drops. Needed to clearly present all 9 member-coded designs as a cohesive, purchasable set.

Concept pairs each member with a boba-cup mascot in their signature color, using cute chibi mouse-ear characters to keep the tone playful and collectible rather than literal member portraits. Grid layout with consistent spacing and a soft dotted background keeps focus on the products while the bubblegum-pink bubble type and onceu wordmark tie it back to brand identity.

Shows character/mascot design skill (not just typography or photo layout) — creating a repeatable, appealing icon system across 9 variations is a stronger illustration exercise.
