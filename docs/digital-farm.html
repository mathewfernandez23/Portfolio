Fresh Fare Farm Digital Ad

A cause-driven digital banner ad for a local farm/food brand; audience is community members who could sign up to help fight food insecurity. Needed to communicate the call-to-action (sign up) and the campaign tie-in (Hunger Action Month) instantly within a small banner format.

Concept pairs a warm, hand-lettered script with the brand's produce-forward avocado icon to keep the tone friendly and community-rooted rather than corporate. A soft peach-and-green palette and a bright chartreuse callout bubble isolate the CTA text so it's the first thing the eye lands on, even at banner scale.

Shows restraint and hierarchy under tight space constraints — a banner ad has far less room to work with than a poster, so getting logo, message, and CTA to all read clearly is a good test of concise design thinking.
