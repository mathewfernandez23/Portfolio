TWICE Mugshot Poster

A high-contrast, meme-style fan poster; audience is the same fanbase but looking for something edgier/funnier than the yearbook version. Needed clear "mugshot" formatting (height chart, ID card) to sell the joke at a glance.

Concept flips the sweet idol image into a tongue-in-cheek "most wanted" bit — black-and-white desaturation and bold stat cards borrow directly from real booking photo conventions for instant recognition. The wordmark "T.W.I.C.E." at the bottom anchors the joke as a branded series.

Demonstrates format literacy — correctly referencing a real-world visual system (police mugshots) and adapting it cleanly is a stronger design exercise than pure decoration.
